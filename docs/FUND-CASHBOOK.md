# Fund and cashbook (development, recorded movements only)
The fund cashbook is a read-only administrator report of actual recorded external movements. It combines capital contributions, investor principal withdrawals, manually recorded distributions, manual customer receipts and reversals, installment receipts and reversals, and actual supplier payments. A supplier invoice is a liability, not a cash outflow. Allocation to an order and a returned-principal credit are internal investor accounting entries, not external cash flows. A receipt reversal is a bookkeeping correction, not necessarily a cash refund.
The period uses inclusive local calendar dates (Asia/Tashkent). Original receipt dates and reversal posting dates are separate. The opening is the sum of all recorded movements before the period; closing = opening + positive movements - negative movements. No opening cash balance is assumed other than recorded history. All amounts are integer UZS, returned as strings. The detailed list is capped at 200 rows but totals cover the entire period.
Important: this is a provisional net-recorded-movements report, NOT a reconciled bank or physical cash balance. The existing distribution event is interpreted as an externally paid distribution for this report only, subject to confirmation; it does not represent earned profit. Other expenses, cash purchase payments, historical opening balances, supplier posting/reversal UI, bank-versus-cash account separation, and historical migration are not complete. Unrecorded movements are absent. Do not use the figure as spendable cash or assume completeness.
The fund overview separately reports installment balances, overdue schedule amounts, supplier obligations, and investor principal balances. Expected profit, investor earned profit, and unactivated contract receivables are not yet complete. No confidential workbook data is committed.
