# Supplier inventory receipts

> **Removed from the app on 2026-11-26 (owner decision).** The suppliers section, its API and the stock receipt against a supplier purchase are gone. The tables described here stay in the database, unused. Stock is now set by a plain adjustment (new quantity and a reason, administrator only): `POST /api/admin/inventory`, table `stock_adjustments` (migration 030).

Purchase obligations, physical stock receipts and supplier payments are separate operations. The administrator selects an existing supplier invoice and an active variant, records the quantity, whole-UZS unit acquisition cost and actual receipt date, and confirms the receipt. A partial receipt is supported; the total recorded receipt value cannot exceed the invoice value. No separate delivery fee is included.
Posting locks the invoice, verifies its remaining receipt value and atomically updates variant stock, inserts a stock movement, records the receipt and audit event. Request keys prevent duplicate postings. Receiving goods does not alter supplier debt or cashbook movements. Existing sale logic decreases stock.
The stock page reports current counts, received/unreceived invoice value and receipt history. It uses a bounded list of existing catalog variants. Purchase order line quantities, lot-level sales cost allocation (FIFO or other policy), inventory corrections/returns, full pagination and historical opening stock reconciliation remain pending. Posting is disabled by default. The receipt cost is not automatically used as the sale cost snapshot until the cost allocation policy is approved.
