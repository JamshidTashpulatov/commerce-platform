# Investor-only allocation — corrected business direction
The business has no separate profit share. Each financed sale is associated with the investors who funded it. Incoming customer repayments attributable to that sale must be attributed only to those participating investors, rather than the entire investor pool. Recovered principal and profit must be recorded separately. The confirmed initial payment is also a return of investor principal, not profit. The intended workflow funds the acquisition cost first, then credits the actual initial receipt to the participating investors. Markup continues to use the remaining financed cost; the acquisition funding and outstanding principal are distinct. Confirmed rule: recovered principal can fund subsequent purchases before the original installment contract is settled. Once the actual receipt is recorded and its principal allocation is posted to the relevant investor, that amount is released from outstanding allocated capital into available capital. Scheduled or unreceived payments do not release capital. The previous proposed business-share model is superseded; historical coefficients must not be interpreted as a business share.
## Monthly receipts

The system determines principal and profit. Only investors who financed the purchased product participate, in proportion to their original invested amounts. Repayments and subsequent use of recovered capital do not change these weights.

For new monthly receipts on fully funded, sold orders, the receipt transaction stores the original funding amount per participating investor. Retrying the receipt preserves the same snapshot. A reversal retains the evidence. Existing receipts and incomplete funding histories require reconciliation before posting. The current order creation workflow has one SKU line per order. Multi-product ordering will require funding attribution per line.

For cumulative receipts R, financed principal P and markup M, the cumulative principal credit is round(R × P / (P + M)) in whole UZS. The rest is realized profit. Each posting records the increase since the previous cumulative credits in the same transaction as the receipt. The final payment reconciles exactly. Recovered principal becomes available capital. Profit remains separate from external contributions and distributions.

The implementation uses highest averages with stable investor-ID ties for cumulative whole-UZS shares. It never reduces a previous credit when a small payment arrives. Each principal credit is capped by the capital still due to that investor. The initial manually posted shares must agree with original weights to within one UZS; material differences and unreconciled historical monthly receipts require review. The supported path starts with a sold, fully funded order and fully posted initial returns. Admin reversal verifies remaining capital and cumulative allocations under locks. Recorded distributions require review because their source receipt is not tracked. Reversals post dated counter-entries. A discrepancy can stay open until an actual recovery or authorized correction is documented. Closing a review does not transfer money or change a ledger. Legacy unallocated receipts retain their existing correction flow.

## Capital availability (confirmed direction)
Available principal = contributed principal - external capital withdrawals - original allocated principal + posted, unreversed recovered principal. Outstanding allocated principal = original allocated principal - posted, unreversed recovered principal. Keep profit separate until its reinvestment policy is approved. Reusing recovered principal must not count as a new external capital contribution. Reversals and subsequent reservations must be reconciled atomically to prevent double spending; Confirmed on 2026-11-25: when the recovered principal has already been reused, or the investor has a recorded profit distribution, the reversal stays blocked. The admin opens a discrepancy and closes it with documented evidence. The system does not post a negative investor balance or withhold from later receipts.
