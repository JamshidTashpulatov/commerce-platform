# Profit allocation — confirmed direction, draft implementation
Each contract can have its own approved investor-pool share. The remaining approved distributable profit belongs to the business. Capital repayment is not profit. Do not derive distributable profit directly from sales revenue or automatically recognize the full contractual markup as earned cash profit.
The pure calculation helper accepts an approved nonnegative profit amount and an approved investor share in basis points. It floors the investor-pool amount to whole UZS and gives the remainder to the business so totals reconcile. This rounding policy is proposed, pending approval.
The source of distributable profit, timing of recognition, assignment of investor-pool shares to multiple investors, historical coefficients, losses, reversals and capital release still require reconciliation and approval. Preserve historical contracts as recorded. The helper does not post accounting entries or trigger distributions. No actual workbook data or confidential rates are committed.
