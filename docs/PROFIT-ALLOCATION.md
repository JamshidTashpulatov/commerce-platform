# Investor-only allocation — corrected business direction
The business has no separate profit share. Each financed sale is associated with the investors who funded it. Incoming customer repayments attributable to that sale must be attributed only to those participating investors, rather than the entire investor pool. Recovered principal and profit must be recorded separately. Recovered funds can be reused for subsequent purchases. The previous proposed business-share model is superseded; historical coefficients must not be interpreted as a business share.
## Proposed calculation, not yet approved
The pure preview helper apportions separately supplied principal and profit amounts in proportion to the funding weights of the participating investors. It allocates whole UZS using the largest-remainder method and stable investor-ID tie breaking. This is a proposed proportional rule, not a confirmed policy; the actual rules for splitting payments into principal/profit, individual investor shares, whether initial payments are eligible, rounding across repeated receipts, and when capital becomes reusable remain to be approved. No automatic recognition, fund movement, posting or reinvestment is implemented by this helper.
Production posting requires a frozen funding snapshot, idempotent receipt-linked allocation ledger, cumulative reconciliation, reversals, and access controls. Preserve original historical data; do not recalculate historical contracts with new terms. No private workbook records or rates are stored in this public repository.
